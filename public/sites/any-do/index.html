Questions people ask about Any.do

What is Any.do?

Any.do is a to-do list, planner, calendar and shared task board. Small teams use it to plan and track work on shared boards, families use it as a shared organizer, and individuals use it as their personal to-do list. It runs on iPhone, Android, Mac, Windows and the web, and more than 50 million people use it.

Does Any.do work with ChatGPT and Claude?

Yes. Any.do has an official MCP connector that works with ChatGPT, Claude, Codex and other MCP-compatible AI agents. Once connected, your AI can create tasks, set due dates and reminders, update and complete tasks, and read your lists and boards. The connector is free on every plan.

How do I connect an AI agent to Any.do?

In ChatGPT, Claude or Codex, add a custom connector with the address mcp.any.do/mcp, then sign in to Any.do and approve access. After that, ask your AI to add, update or find tasks. You can disconnect at any time.

Can teammates use different AI assistants on the same board?

Yes. Each teammate connects the AI they prefer, and every change lands on the same shared board, where the whole team can see it.

How much does Any.do Teams cost?

Any.do Teams costs $4.99 per member per month billed yearly, or $7.99 per member billed monthly. Every team starts with a 14-day free trial, and no credit card is needed.

Is Any.do a good alternative to Trello, Asana or monday.com for small teams?

Any.do is built for small teams that want something simpler: shared boards with board, calendar and table views, automations, comments, WhatsApp reminders and an AI connector, starting at $4.99 per member per month. Teams can import their existing work and get set up in minutes.

Is Any.do free for personal use?

Yes. The personal to-do list, planner, calendar and reminders are free forever. Any.do Premium adds features such as WhatsApp reminders and location reminders for $4.99 per month billed yearly.

Can my family share lists in Any.do?

Yes. Any.do Family lets up to 4 people share lists and a grocery list that updates in real time, and assign tasks to each other. It costs $8.33 per month billed yearly for the whole family.
